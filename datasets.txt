RESOURCE INVENTORY
==================

This study uses no public machine-learning dataset. The matched clinical records
that support the clinical-correlation analysis are de-identified and are held
under the access terms of the governing ethics approval reported in the
manuscript; they are not redistributed with this repository. The
clinical-correlation step reads your access-controlled copy of them; no other
step needs them.

This repository ships no cohort data. The clinical-correlation analysis reads a
de-identified, access-controlled records file that is supplied at run time
(`python -m livewell.panel correlate --records PATH`, or `cohort.records` in the
experiment configuration); nothing is downloaded and no cohort is produced in
process. The loader layout is documented in `livewell/hatchery/cohort.py`, and
the values computed from the records are the study estimates reported in the
manuscript.

The only externally hosted resources are the reference pages for the three
commercial hepatocellular-carcinoma cell lines named in the device
characterization. Each link below returned HTTP 200 on verification.

HepG2
  Cellosaurus  https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_0027
  ATCC HB-8065 https://www.atcc.org/products/hb-8065

Huh7
  Cellosaurus  https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_0336
  JCRB0403     https://cellbank.nibiohn.go.jp/~cellbank/en/search_res_det.cgi?RNO=JCRB0403

HCCLM3
  Cellosaurus  https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_6832

ACCESS NOTES
============

Cell lines ship under the supplying repository material-transfer terms.

The patient-derived-organoid specimens and the paired clinical course are
de-identified and were never disclosed in identifiable form. They are held under
the ethics approval reported in the manuscript and are not redistributable; none
of the software checks distributed here require them, and no cohort records are
shipped with or produced by this repository.
